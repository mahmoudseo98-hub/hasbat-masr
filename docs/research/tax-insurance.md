# Research record — Income tax (freelancers) & social insurance (as of 2026-11-21)

## Income tax brackets — Law 91/2005 Art. 8 as replaced by Law 7/2024 (OFFICIAL)
https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2024-03/law_no.7-2024.pdf — published 2024-02-21; unchanged by Law 151/2026
(https://eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2026-08/law.no_.151.of_.2026.pdf).
0–40k 0% | 40–55k 10% | 55–70k 15% | 70–200k 20% | 200–400k 22.5% | 400k–1.2M 25% | >1.2M 27.5%.
Bracket elimination by total income: >600k lose 0% bracket; >700k lose 10%; >800k lose 15%; >900k lose 20%;
>1.2M: 25% on first 1.2M then 27.5%. (Deloitte text confirms reading.)
Net income rounded DOWN to nearest 10 EGP. Check values: 600,000 → 124,750; 600,010 → 128,752.50 (Global Kinect).
Personal exemption 20,000 = salary income only (Art. 13; SECONDARY reading) — NOT applied to freelancers.

## Professionals (Part 4, Art. 32–35) (OFFICIAL)
Costs: actual documented costs with regular books; flat 10% of gross revenue without books (Art. 35; ETA post 2024-09-22).
Law 150/2026 (in force 2026-07-29) obliges all professionals to keep books (except Law 6/2025 regime) — whether the 10%
fallback is still applied is UNVERIFIED. Withholding 5% by Egyptian payers (Art. 70). Return due by 31 March.
VAT for professionals: 10% schedule tax; exported services 0% — out of scope for this calculator.

## Simplified regime — Law 6/2025 Art. 10 (OFFICIAL), in force 2025-03-01
Rate on whole turnover: <500k 0.4% | 500k–<2M 0.5% | 2M–<3M 0.75% | 3M–<10M 1% | 10M–20M 1.5%.
Professional activities eligible; excluded: professional consulting with ≥90% of turnover from 1–2 clients.
Requires application (Form 1/10), e-invoice/e-receipt, 5-year lock-in. Tolerance up to +20% over 20M once per 5 years.
https://www.eta.gov.eg/sites/default/files/2025-02/law_no.6.of_.2025.pdf ; EY alert (SECONDARY).

## Social insurance — Law 148/2019 (OFFICIAL law text on nosi.gov.eg)
2026: employer 18.75% (12 old-age + 1 end-of-service + 1.5 injury + 3.25 sickness + 1 unemployment),
employee 11% (9 old-age + 1 end-of-service + 1 sickness).
2027 (from 1 Jan): +1% split equally on old-age → employer 19.25%, employee 11.5% (law text Art. 19; SECONDARY reports).
Insurable wage limits: 2025 2,300–14,500 | 2026 2,700–16,700 (OFFICIAL, NOSI 2025-11-30
https://www.nosi.gov.eg/ar/News/Pages/2025-11-30.aspx) | 2027 3,200–19,300 (SECONDARY: elwatannews 8397469, NOSI chairman).
Business owners/self-employed: 21% of chosen income within limits (Art. 19(2)); 2027 rate UNVERIFIED → not modelled.
Minimum pension 2026: 1,755 (65% of min wage). Outside Law 148 (not modelled): Emergency Fund 1% employer, Martyrs' Fund 0.05%.
