# Research record — Laptop customs & residential electricity (as of 2026-11-21)

## Laptops (HS 8471.30, line 8471300090)
- Import duty 0% — UNVERIFIED on the official tariff (gomruky.com/customs/laptops, arabtaxcalc agree).
- VAT 14% (Law 67/2016) on customs value (CIF, Customs Law 207/2020 Art. 16) + duty + other fees — PwC (SECONDARY).
- Phone governance system (38.5%) does NOT cover laptops/tablets — elbalad 2025-01-01 (SECONDARY).
- Traveller: personal portable computers are personal effects (OFFICIAL, customs.gov.eg TravelAndTourism?travelAndTourismId=1);
  new items allowance EGP 15,000 per person, taxes due on the excess (OFFICIAL, same page).
- Courier/postal flat rate: NOT FOUND (customs said 2026-10-25 it is still studying e-commerce parcels). Not modelled.
- FX: CBE rate on the declaration date (Law 207/2020 Art. 16).
